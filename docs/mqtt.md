# Legacy Mysa MQTT protocol

The maintained MQTT authorization findings, app-derived wire format, examples,
and passive diagnostic instructions now live in the standalone client
repository:
[Legacy Mysa MQTT protocol](https://github.com/SokolskyNikita/mysa-client/blob/main/docs/mqtt.md).

The findings describe the inspected identity and hardware; they do not
establish that MQTT is unavailable for every Mysa account or device. Mysa Local
has no runtime MQTT implementation.
